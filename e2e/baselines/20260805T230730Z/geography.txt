# The world saves are stale (see save-vintage.txt), so the T0 spatial table —
# mean x, the x histogram, east-quarter occupancy — has no T1 counterpart and
# CANNOT be produced. Nothing in the archive substitutes for it: every genome
# the archive stored was captured at the moment its organism reached the export
# edge, so their positions pile up at x = S-W = 1960 and describe the edge, not
# the world. A sample of 1499 archived genomes puts the median x at 1960.
#
# What does survive is the mod's own edge instrumentation, logged once per
# simulated minute for the whole night (CrossingStats.cs):
#
#   stripEntries — an organism crossing INTO the border strip from inside;
#   crossings    — an organism passing the playable square edge itself.
#
# Divided by the population, that is how hard the living population is pressing
# on its eastern boundary — the geographic question the spatial table answered
# at a single instant, asked instead over 12.5 hours.
