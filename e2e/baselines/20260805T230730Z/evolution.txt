The evolution reading of the migrant stream, done honestly.

Two facts about `genomeHash` shape everything below, and both come from
contracts/genome-hash.md §4: the projection it hashes is EXACTLY the 35-gene
array plus the brain's nodes and synapses plus the game version. It does not
include generation, speciesID or isMutant, which sit one level up in the .bb8.

So:
  * every gene value and every brain measurement below is EXACT — it is
    literally the thing the hash covers, read back from the archived genome;
  * generation and speciesID are the values of the FIRST organism ever recorded
    carrying that genome. They date when the genome first appeared, which is
    what makes them useful, but they are not a per-hop measurement and they are
    never averaged as if they were.
