sidecar-1.log, sidecar-3.log and archive.log all end mid-record and are then
padded with NUL bytes to the next block boundary. That is the filesystem
signature of a process whose pages were never flushed — the WSL VM or the
machine went away underneath them rather than each service exiting. It also
means the last few log lines of each file may be missing; the JOURNALS and
the ARCHIVE LEDGER are fsync'd records and are not affected.

Slot 2 was out of the ring for three hours (08:46Z to 11:47Z by its own
traffic; 09:49Z to 11:46Z by the relay's view of its mod). The gap between
the two — an hour in which the relay still believed the mod was connected
while no organism crossed — is what a suspended machine looks like from
here: the socket stayed nominally open and nothing came down it. Nothing on
this side can confirm that; it is the shape of the evidence, not a fact.
